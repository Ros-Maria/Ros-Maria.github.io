Voice-Based Phishing Detection

End-to-end signal-level vishing detection using MFCCs, log-Mel spectrograms, and a CNN (F1 = 0.91). Applied Grad-CAM to highlight phishing-specific spectral regions. Outperformed SVM, Logistic Regression, and Gradient Boosting baselines.

ML Attack on Hardware PUF

Simulated hybrid current mirror inverter PUF in HSPICE. Generated 100k challenge-response pairs under process variation. Evaluated SVM, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, KNN — best attack accuracy 92.55% with SVM. PCA & UMAP for feature analysis.

Fast Local Replanning for Single-Query Paths

Novel path planning combining RRT* with local collision-triggered replanning instead of global restart. Demonstrated improved timing efficiency and 100% success rate over standard RRT* and RRTX across 10 independent runs.
